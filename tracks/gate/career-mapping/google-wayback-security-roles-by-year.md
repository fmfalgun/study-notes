**Read this first:** this is every posting Wayback happened to archive, not every Google opening -- see
`google-wayback-analysis.md` for what that does and does not support. Titles are re-cased from URL slugs
(so punctuation and some capitalisation are lost); classification is a keyword heuristic on the title.
