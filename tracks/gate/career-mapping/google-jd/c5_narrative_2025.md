# Google security job descriptions, 2025 description-capture year (findings/2025.md, regenerated with the corrected degree-line rule)

**Scope and limits, read first.** Postings are only those Wayback archived; this is not a census of Google's roles. 2019-2022 postings are title-based only (no recoverable descriptions); descriptions cover postings archived from mid-2023 (capture years 2023-2026). This file covers the **2025** capture year: **194** postings (security_engineering 71, threat_intel_and_response 67, sales_and_consulting 30, other_security 15 W, physical_corporate_security 11 W). W = n<30 (weak, anecdotal). physical_corporate_security is NOT cybersecurity and is never mixed into cybersecurity numbers below. The text is what the page said at its capture date, not necessarily when the role opened. Every count is "postings containing it / postings in the group" and is tagged with its METHOD: **[regex]** = a documented regex on the raw section text (Table "Regex metrics", identical for every year); **[phrase-term]** = the data-driven phrase extraction (degree-requirement lines removed first, words must be adjacent inside one clause, see c4_method.md and c5_method.md). Causes are inference.

**Change since the pilot draft:** the degree-line rule now drops only lines that ARE the degree requirement, so requirement lines that mention a degree only as an alternative ("... or 1 year of experience with an advanced degree") are kept and counted. Effect: security_engineering required python is [[python|minimum_qualifications|security_engineering]] (pilot draft: 20/71) and [[re:python_req|security_engineering]]; c++ [[c++|minimum_qualifications|security_engineering]] versus [[re:cpp_req|security_engineering]] (the phrase term misses "c/c++" written without spaces, see the reconciliation table).
