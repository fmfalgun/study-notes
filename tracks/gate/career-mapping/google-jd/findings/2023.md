## Qualitative notes (what the term counts miss)


Scope: postings are only those Wayback archived; captures from July to December 2023. 2019-2022 postings are title-based only. These notes read 8 of the 26 postings captured in 2023 (security_engineering 3, threat_intel_and_response 3, sales_and_consulting 1, other_security 1). n=26 is weak: nothing here is a share or a trend, only what these 8 postings look like. Quotes are verbatim, at most 25 words, each with (job_id, theme).

**1. The requirement blocks are less standardised than in later years.** One security-engineer posting lists bare skills: "Experience in Threat Modeling" (80182026012893894, security_engineering) and "Experience in Code Reviews" (80182026012893894, security_engineering), with no years and no degree line. A software-engineer posting already uses the later template: "8 years of experience in software development, and with data structures/algorithms." (79518963861463750, security_engineering).

**2. Parsed sections can be empty.** The head-of-customer-success posting has an empty responsibilities section and empty about text (84478466012914374, other_security), so a term absent from a 2023 posting may be a parse gap; the empty-section line in the section-length table counts these.

**3. Incident-response and detection roles are written by activity, in wording that recurs later.** "Conduct host forensics, network forensics, log analysis, and malware triage in support of incident response investigations." (78684822089671366, threat_intel_and_response). A detection posting: "Participate in a 24/7 global operation that looks after and responds to security events on Google's networks." (105524390365405894, threat_intel_and_response).

**4. Gating.** "US Government Top Secret/Sensitive Compartmented Information Clearance." (141710460480037574, threat_intel_and_response); a sales lead needs "Ability to travel up to 40% of the time as needed." (87672345629467334, sales_and_consulting).

**5. Kernel and research-style engineering.** "Participate in kCTFs, having found and exploited kernel zero-days." (79518963861463750, security_engineering).

**6. Internship postings state the degree being pursued.** "Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Security, or related technical field" (85870200504623814, security_engineering).

**7. Sales/business roles ask for executive access.** "Ability to speak to C-Suite and in particular CISOs and Information Security/Risk business owners." (87672345629467334, sales_and_consulting).
