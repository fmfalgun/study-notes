# Google security job descriptions, 2025 description-capture year (DRAFT findings/2025.md)

**Scope and limits, read first.** Postings are only those Wayback archived; this is not a census of Google's roles. 2019-2022 postings are title-based only (no recoverable descriptions); descriptions cover postings archived from mid-2023 (capture years 2023-2026). This file covers the **2025** capture year: **194** postings (security_engineering 71, threat_intel_and_response 67, sales_and_consulting 30, other_security 15 W, physical_corporate_security 11 W). W = n<30 (weak, anecdotal). physical_corporate_security is NOT cybersecurity and is never mixed into cybersecurity numbers below. The text is what the page said at its capture date (a snapshot), not necessarily when the role was opened. Term counts come from phrase matching (a heuristic, see c4_method.md); causes are inference. Every count is "postings containing the term in that section / postings in the group".

## Headline
1. **Security engineering and threat/response are asked for differently.** Required text in security_engineering leans to programming and software development (python 20/71 (28%), c++ 20/71 (28%), programming languages 19/71 (27%)); in threat_intel_and_response it leans to network forensics 18/67 (27%), incident response 16/67 (24%) and malware 15/67 (22%); in sales_and_consulting to cybersecurity 19/30 (63%), cloud 14/30 (47%) and enterprise/client wording (see Table below).
2. **Degree: a Bachelor's is near-universal, advanced degrees are a preference.** 'Bachelor' appears in the required section of 190/194 (98%) postings. Master's/PhD wording appears in the preferred section of 48/194 (25%), but 34/71 (48%) in security_engineering versus 8/67 (12%) in threat_intel_and_response (regex heuristic, Table "Stated years..." below).
3. **Certifications are a preference, with exceptions.** Certifications appear in the preferred section of 47/194 (24%) postings and in the required section of 5/194 (3%); in threat_intel_and_response the preferred share is 23/67 (34%) and in sales_and_consulting 12/30 (40%).
4. **Experience levels cluster at 5-6 years.** Highest "N years" figure in the required section: 5-6 years for 69/194 (36%), 7 or more for 53/194 (27%), 1-2 for 38/194 (20%). The 1-2 group is mostly security_engineering (22/71, 31%), where the generic software-engineer template is used (see qualitative notes). Sales and consulting is the most senior group: 13/30 at 7+ years.
5. **Some requirements gate who can apply rather than what they know.** 'Security clearance' appears in the required section of 24/194 (12%) postings; 'travel' in the required section of 15/67 (22%) of threat_intel_and_response postings.
6. **What is done (responsibilities) is verb-led and mostly cross-theme:** conduct 57/194 (29%), best practices 45/194 (23%) (both appear almost only in the responsibilities section), design reviews 27/194 (14%), incident response 27/194 (14%).

Preferred: certifications 12/30 (40%), cloud security 12/30 (40%). Done: customer 19/30 (63%), plans 15/30 (50%), opportunities 12/30 (40%).

## Limits (this year file)
- One capture year (2025), 194 postings: no trend can be read from it; a trend needs the same method applied to 2023, 2024, 2026 (2023 has only 26 postings: weak).
- Themes are derived from titles (earlier step) and are not verified job families; at least one sampled security_engineering posting is an AI/ML customer role.
- 15 of 194 postings were read closely; counts describe all 194.
- Distinct (required + responsibilities) texts: 183 of 194; 8 texts repeat; repeated texts count once per posting.
- Term counts: phrase matching, English only, no synonyms beyond simple plural merging; a missing term means the exact word is absent, not that the skill is absent. Generic words dropped from the unigram lists are listed in c4_method.md.
- Descriptions are snapshots at the capture date (not the wording when the posting first appeared). 2019-2022 postings are title-based only.
- Causes are inference and are not stated here.
