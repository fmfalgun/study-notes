# APPENDIX (anecdotal only): 'Qualifications:' snippets on archived Google careers front pages

**Read this first.** This is NOT a sample of security roles and supports NO trend claim. Each archived front-page snapshot of the jobs-results page lists ~20 job cards in whatever order Google's search returned that day; some cards carry a 'Qualifications:' preview (minimum qualifications only, as bullets). I kept the cards whose TITLE the project's security classifier flags as cybersecurity. Postings/titles here are whichever happened to sit on a front page; 2019-2022 postings are title-based only elsewhere in this study, and these snippets do not change that. Source: already-captured snapshots in scraper-data (offline, no new requests).
