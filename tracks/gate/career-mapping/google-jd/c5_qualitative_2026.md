These notes read 12 of the 89 postings captured in 2026 (security_engineering 4, threat_intel_and_response 3, sales_and_consulting 2, other_security 2, physical_corporate_security 1). ## What the role asks for that the term counts miss

**1. AI appears as both a skill to have and a thing to secure.** As a skill: "Experience developing AI agents and building tooling for internal developers." (112422480202605254, security_engineering); "Experience as an international expert in the application of AI/ML to cybersecurity challenges." (105122619300160198, security_engineering). Even a programme-management role asks "Experience using AI and seek opportunities to use AI/ML technologies to lead improvements across programs." (73986696906973894, other_security). As a topic: "Build high-fidelity prototypes simulating agentic AI to test privacy and security concepts in real-world scenarios" (143876826572169926, security_engineering) describes a UX role, so AI counts also reach non-security-engineering work carrying the security_engineering label.

**2. The generic software-engineer template still appears under security titles.** "5 years of experience with software development in one or more programming languages." (112422480202605254, security_engineering); "Write product or system development code." (96435610394206918, security_engineering); its preferred list names "Master's degree or PhD in Computer Science or related technical fields." (96435610394206918, security_engineering).

**3. The security-engineer template covers roles of different kinds.** "5 years of experience with security assessments or security design reviews or threat modeling." (95289251511313094, security_engineering) heads a posting whose responsibilities are investigations: "Perform investigations on a wide variety of security and privacy events from various sources to determine whether they pose a threat to Google." (95289251511313094, security_engineering). The same requirement block heads an incident-response consultant posting: "Must possess an active Top Secret security clearance." (95624077397369542, threat_intel_and_response). The requirement lines say little about the work; the responsibilities do.

**4. Incident-response consulting is written by activity, in wording reused across postings.** "Conduct host forensics, network forensics, log analysis, and malware triage in support of incident response investigations" (79030552585216710, threat_intel_and_response) and (95624077397369542, threat_intel_and_response) use the same sentence. Gates: "Ability to travel up to 30% of the time as required." (79030552585216710, threat_intel_and_response).

**5. Offensive-security consulting puts certifications in the REQUIRED section.** "Certifications related to offensive security, such as OSCE, OSEP, OSEE, OSCP, CCSAS, CCT INF or relevant SANS courses." (81211251094364870, threat_intel_and_response) is a minimum-qualification line here (elsewhere certifications are mostly preferred). The role is client consulting: "Perform offensive technical cyber security engagements of varied nature." (81211251094364870, threat_intel_and_response).

**6. Theme labels come from title keywords and are sometimes off (2 of 12 read).** An operational-technology operations role is labelled sales_and_consulting: "Own regional vulnerability and patch management programs." (84451641347973830, sales_and_consulting), with requirements about building operations. A UX engineer is labelled security_engineering (note 1). A red-team consulting posting is labelled threat_intel_and_response (defensible: offensive and defensive consulting sit together).

**7. Sales roles are quota jobs.** "Deliver against quota and achieve or exceed strategic business and growth goals while forecasting and reporting your territory’s business." (72252590439441094, sales_and_consulting).

**8. Program-management and compliance roles ask for security only as context.** "Experience identifying and mitigating compliance risks." (108826697075172038, other_security).

**9. Physical security keeps a facilities vocabulary and language gates.** "Experience with physical security technologies, including CCTV, access control, incident management systems, and security operations centers." (91252549188756166, physical_corporate_security); "Ability to communicate in English and German fluently to support local stakeholders." (91252549188756166, physical_corporate_security). Physical/corporate security is not cybersecurity; n=3 in 2026 (W).

**10. Research-level roles use a different bar.** A distinguished-engineer posting requires "Experience in research and innovation including publications in relevant venues (e.g., S&P, CCS, USENIX Security, NeurIPS, ICML), patents, or industry-adopted technologies." (105122619300160198, security_engineering) and a PhD-or-equivalent degree line; counts of years and degree hide this.
