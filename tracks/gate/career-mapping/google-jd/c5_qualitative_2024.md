# C5 qualitative notes, 2024 description-capture year (13 postings read in full, seed 2024, listed in c5_sample_2024.txt)

Scope: postings are only those Wayback archived. 2019-2022 postings are title-based only; descriptions cover postings archived from mid-2023. These notes read 13 of the 100 postings captured in 2024 (security_engineering 4, threat_intel_and_response 3, sales_and_consulting 2, other_security 2, physical_corporate_security 2). A sample of 13 illustrates; it proves no share. Quotes are verbatim, at most 25 words, each with (job_id, theme).

## What the role asks for that the term counts miss

**1. Two requirement templates sit behind many "security engineering" counts.** A generic software-engineer template ("software development ... data structures") and a security-engineer template ("security assessments ... security protocols ... coding in general purpose languages"). Term counts for software development, programming languages, python or java in security_engineering therefore partly count which template a posting uses.
- Software-engineer template under a security title: "5 years of experience with software development in one or more programming languages, and with data structures/algorithms." (78793268778672838, security_engineering)
- Same posting, responsibilities that are generic engineering work: "Write and test product or system development code." (78793268778672838, security_engineering)
- Security-engineer template scaled by seniority (5, 8 and 10 years appear in the sampled postings): "10 years of experience with security assessments or security design reviews or threat modeling." (97575005541278406, security_engineering)
- Its responsibilities are leadership of long programmes: "Drive large, technically complex, cross-functional, multi-quarter, and multi-year security efforts that engineer-away common risks and vulnerability classes." (97575005541278406, security_engineering)

**2. Theme labels come from title keywords and are sometimes off (3 of 13 read).**
- A customer-support engineer labelled security_engineering: "Work as part of a team of engineers/consultants that globally ensure 24 hour customer support." (133807659284341446, security_engineering) with "Ability to work non-standard hours and differing rotations/shifts." (133807659284341446, security_engineering).
- An industrial-control-systems cyber posting labelled physical_corporate_security (theme is physical/corporate, not cyber): "3 years of experience in industrial control systems (ICS) and operational technology (OT) security." (139450895942197958, physical_corporate_security). Its requirement block is the security-engineer template, so it would read as security_engineering by content.
- A Trust and Safety analyst role labelled other_security whose duties read as detection and response: "Search for anomalous activity via manual hunting at Alphabet scale." (75224414351696582, other_security).

**3. Vulnerability research and malware analysis are described by activity.** "Find and exploit vulnerabilities affecting Google Cloud." (133245967225758406, security_engineering); "Perform reverse engineering, analyze, and detect malware at scale." (129661102509171398, threat_intel_and_response). The preferred line "Experience as a vulnerability researcher (e.g., vulnerability write-ups, conference talks, etc)." (133245967225758406, security_engineering) asks for public output, not a tool.

**4. Gates that are not skills.** "Active US Government Top Secret/Sensitive Compartmentalized Information (TS/SCI) security clearance with polygraph." (128513480771674822, threat_intel_and_response) and shift/rotation availability (note 2). Sales: "Ability to travel up to 50% of the time as required." (85794657230824134, sales_and_consulting).

**5. Sales and consulting are quota or customer-delivery jobs.** "7 years of experience in a sales role in the enterprise software or cloud space." (85794657230824134, sales_and_consulting); "Cloud or Security industry certifications." (96599479318127302, sales_and_consulting) as a preferred item; "Travel to customer sites, conferences, and other related events as required, acting as a public advocate for Google Cloud." (96599479318127302, sales_and_consulting).

**6. Digital-forensics and incident roles are written in the same security-engineer template.** "5 years of experience with Digital Forensics, Incident Response or Incident Management." (138948966602416838, threat_intel_and_response), next to the standard "5 years of coding" line; a preferred list names compliance and privacy frameworks, not only technical skills.

**7. Adjacent roles list security as one preferred item.** A product-manager posting asks only "Experience with Cybersecurity." (121655276993422022, other_security) as preferred; a physical-security programme role asks for data skills: "Experience with SQL, dashboarding, and data visualization." (78486981876556486, physical_corporate_security).
