Go-To-Market Business Development Lead, Security and Compliance Solutions

Advanced

Experience owning outcomes and decision making, solving ambiguous problems and influencing stakeholders; deep expertise in domain.

  • linkCopy link

Minimum qualifications:

  • Experience in collaborating across organizational boundaries, building relationships, and developing ideas to achieve organizational goals.
  • Experience in communicating topics to an audience with various functional expertise.
  • Experience in a customer-facing role in the enterprise space and translating customer needs into solutions.
  • Ability to travel up to 40% of the time as needed.

Preferred qualifications:

  • Experience in Go-To-Market (GTM) strategy planning and execution, business development or technical solutions within the security and cloud domains.
  • Experience with security related matters, as they relate to risk management, cloud adoption, security operation center, and enterprise architectures.
  • Ability to collaborate effectively across organizational boundaries, build relationships, and develop ideas to achieve organizational goals.
  • Ability to speak to C-Suite and in particular CISOs and Information Security/Risk business owners.
  • Excellent business skills (e.g., financial accounting, ROI, TCO, budget planning, organizational design).
  • Excellent presentation skills including public speaking, meeting facilitation, and white boarding.

About the job

As the Head of Security Solution Practice for one or more EMEA Sub regions, you will work with a team of sales specialists and go-to-market specialists, bringing your experience and thought leadership to help create a vision and set and execute a go-to-market strategy for the region. You will report to the Security and Compliance EMEA Practice Leader. The Solution portfolio includes, but is not limited to Google Cloud Platform (GCP) Security Foundation, Threat Ready with Mandiant, transforming Security Operations and includes technologies like Security Command Center Premium, Chronicle, Mandiant Advantage Platform, Virus Total, Security AI Workbench, and Mandiant consulting services.

Google Cloud accelerates organizations’ ability to digitally transform their business with the best infrastructure, platform, industry solutions and expertise. We deliver enterprise-grade solutions that leverage Google’s cutting-edge technology – all on the cleanest cloud in the industry. Customers in more than 200 countries and territories turn to Google Cloud as their trusted partner to enable growth and solve their most critical business problems.

Responsibilities

  • Identify and prioritize security solution for EMEA and sub-regions and contribute to new logo acquisition leveraging security as a door opener with CISOs.
  • Plan and execute demand generation campaigns, opportunities progression initiatives, roadshows, and driving adoption.
  • Define and execute scalable motions to support the technical teams and partners to go live and prove value to customers.
  • Work closely with global solution and product management and functions to develop new offerings and business plays.
  • Collaborate with the regional business leaders, the GCP cloud sellers, and the security specialists technical and promoting organizations.
