## Formulas (quant)
- % change: new = old × (1 ± p/100). Successive changes a% then b%: net = a + b + ab/100 (%).
- Ratio: a:b = c:d ⇒ ad = bc. Mixture (alligation): quantity ratio = (c₂ − m) : (m − c₁), where m is the mean price.
- Average speed over equal distances = 2xy/(x + y). Speed (km/h) × 5/18 = m/s.
- Time and work: combined rate = 1/a + 1/b; time together = ab/(a + b).
- Profit % = (SP − CP)/CP × 100. SI = PRT/100. CI = P[(1 + r/100)ⁿ − 1].
- ⁿPᵣ = n!/(n − r)!, ⁿCᵣ = n!/(r!(n − r)!).
- Train crossing a platform: time = (train + platform length)/speed.

## Worked examples (all recomputed in code)

**E1: Successive percentage.** Price up 20%, then down 20%: net = 1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96, so a **4% loss** (96 per 100).

**E2: Time and work.** A takes 10 days, B takes 15 days: together 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6 per day, so **6 days**.

**E3: Average speed.** 40 km/h one way and 60 km/h back (same distance): **48 km/h**, not 50.

**E4: Interest.** On ₹1000: SI at 5% for 3 years = **₹150**; SI at 10% for 2 years = **₹200**; CI at 10% for 2 years = **₹210** (the extra ₹10 is interest on interest).

**E5: Profit.** Mark up 25% over cost, then give a 20% discount on the marked price: 1.25 × 0.8 = 1.00, so **0% profit**.

**E6: Mixture.** 2 kg at ₹30/kg mixed with 3 kg at ₹50/kg: mean price = (60 + 150)/5 = **₹42/kg**.

**E7: Counting.** ⁵P₃ = 5!/2! = **60**; ⁵P₂ = 5!/3! = **20**; ⁵C₂ = **10**.

**E8: Train.** 150 m train, 250 m platform, 54 km/h = 15 m/s: time = 400/15 = **26.67 s**.
